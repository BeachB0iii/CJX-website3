CJX Realtor Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy describes the current CJX Realtor Toolkit iOS and iPadOS app. It does not describe other pages or services on this website.

Information used within the app

The app uses the calculator values you enter to generate estimates. Agent details entered in My Info, including name, brokerage, license number, phone, and email, are saved locally using the device's app settings storage. Calculator values are held within the app's current session.

The app does not require an account. The app does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics, and does not send calculator inputs or agent details to the developer's servers.

Reports you choose to share

Exported PDFs and images can contain your calculator values and agent details. Reports are generated on your device. When you use the system share sheet, you choose whether and where to save or share them. The recipient or service you choose may handle those reports under its own privacy practices.

Managing your information

You can edit or clear the details in My Info. Removing the app removes its local app data from that installation; reports saved or shared elsewhere are separate copies and must be managed where they were saved. Device backups are governed by your device and Apple settings.

Support requests

If you contact app support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive client or financial information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.
