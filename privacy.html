Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version: when you fill out a form on this site, I use what you send to contact you about your real estate question. I don’t sell your information or hand my contact list to anyone. The few companies that help me run the site are listed below, along with what each one receives.

Who this covers

This policy applies to cjxrealty.com, operated by Christian Jerman doing business as CJX Real Estate. I’m a licensed Arizona real estate agent (license SA706224000) brokered by HomeSmart. “I” and “me” in this policy mean Christian Jerman and CJX Real Estate.

What I collect

Information you give me. The forms on this site ask for some or all of the following:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • A property address, if you ask for a home valuation
  • The best time to reach you, if you ask about a listing
  • Whatever you write in the message box

Information collected automatically. When you submit a form, the page you were on is saved with it. If you arrived from an ad or a tagged link, the ad click ID and campaign tags in that link may be saved with your inquiry too, so I can tell which marketing is working. Like most websites, the hosting provider also processes basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.

How I use it

  • To reply to your inquiry by email, phone, or text
  • To send you the market updates you signed up for
  • To keep a record of our conversation while I’m helping you
  • To understand which pages and ads bring people to the site

That’s all. I don’t use your information for anything unrelated to helping you with real estate.

Who else handles it

I don’t sell your personal information, and I don’t give or rent my contact list to anyone. Running a website and a real estate business does mean a few outside companies handle information along the way. Here is each one, and what it receives.

  • Follow Up Boss (operated by Zillow, Inc.). This is the contact manager where your form submission is stored so I can follow up. It receives everything you enter in a form. Follow Up Boss is a Zillow brand, and Zillow handles the information under its own privacy notice. If you also have your own account with Zillow, Zillow may treat matching contact details as information it shares with you as its own customer, under that notice.
  • Google Ads. This site loads a Google advertising tag. It tells Google that a page was visited or that an inquiry form was sent, so I can see whether my ads are working. Google receives your IP address and the page address, and may set or read cookies in your browser. I don’t send Google your name, email, phone number, or message. Google uses what it receives under its own policies, described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Fonts and YouTube. The site’s typefaces load from Google, and video thumbnails and the listing tour video load from YouTube. To deliver those files, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details. The fonts don’t set cookies, and the tour video uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player.
  • Cloudflare. Cloudflare hosts and secures this site and delivers its icon files, so it processes your IP address and basic browser details for every page you load.

Links on this site to Google Calendar booking, Google reviews, and social media take you to those companies’ own sites, which have their own privacy policies.

I may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to my brokerage, HomeSmart, and the other professionals in a transaction (such as a title company or lender) when you’ve asked me to help you buy or sell and it’s needed to do that.

Cookies and your advertising choices

I don’t set cookies of my own. The Google advertising tag may. You have three ways to limit it:

  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser. When this site sees that signal, it doesn’t load the Google advertising tag at all.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Turn off ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Some state privacy laws treat ad measurement tags like this one as “sharing” information for advertising. The Global Privacy Control setting above is how to opt out of that on this site. The site works the same whichever you choose.

Emails, calls, and texts

If you give me your email or phone number, I may contact you about your inquiry by email, call, or text. Message and data rates may apply to texts, and how often I message depends on our conversation. You can stop at any time: reply STOP to a text, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or tell me by phone at 480-531-7991.

I don’t share your phone number or your consent to receive texts with anyone for their marketing. Calls and texts may be sent through my contact manager and its messaging provider.

How long I keep it

I keep your contact information for as long as we’re working together or you’re receiving updates from me, and afterward as long as real estate record-keeping rules require. If you ask me to delete it, I will, unless I’m legally required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information I have about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state’s privacy laws. To make a request, call or text 480-531-7991 or send a note through the contact form.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection, and form submissions go directly to my contact manager. No website can promise perfect security, so please don’t send sensitive details such as Social Security or bank account numbers through these forms.

Children

This site is meant for adults. I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
