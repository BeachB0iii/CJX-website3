CJX Realtor Toolkit Support

CJX Realtor Toolkit is a free app for exploring mortgage payments, seller proceeds, and fix-and-flip scenarios.

Getting started

Choose Mortgage, Net Sheet, or Fix & Flip at the bottom of the app. Adjust the inputs to match your scenario. Results update as you change the values.

In Mortgage, enable Utilities to add monthly Electric, Pool, Pest, Security, Solar, Water/Trash/Sewer, and Landscaping estimates. Turn Utilities off to remove those costs from the monthly total.

Use Export PDF or Save Image to open the sharing options. Tap the person icon to edit the agent details included in exported reports.

Help and feedback

Contact: info@cjxrealty.com

For a calculation or export issue, include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, and a description of the issue. Use example figures where possible and avoid sending private client information.

All calculations are estimates based on your inputs. Actual financing terms, expenses, and transaction outcomes may differ. The app does not provide a loan commitment or offer.
